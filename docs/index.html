Replay

Rewind any session. Watch the whole crowd.

Scrub through one playtester's run in the Scene view, or overlay everyone at once and let the heatmap show you where the level really is.

  • Paths, view cones and events, synced to one timeline
  • Combined heatmap across every session in the scene
  • Click any moment to jump straight to it
Scene · Heatmap layer · cell size 1 m
Attention

Know what they noticed. And what they walked right past.

PlaySense checks what's really on screen, in view and unblocked, and tracks what the player focuses on and for how long.

  • Share of players who looked at each object
  • Repeat looks flagged as reinspections
  • Works in first and third person

All Sessions

Twenty playtests. One clear answer.

Every playthrough is a session. PlaySense adds them all up, so "I think players ignore the ability" becomes "14 of 20 used it, and 70% of kills were still gunfire."

  • % of players for every event you log
  • Breakdowns by any detail: weapon, shot type, choice
  • How far players got, level by level
  • Export to CSV for your spreadsheet

        Modular
        
Take what you need. Delete the rest.

        
Every feature is its own module. Remove one and its components, dashboard tab and replay layer go with it. Need something we don't record? Write your own module in a few lines.

      

      

        

          
Core
Session clock, scenes, events, storage, the Dashboard and the Welcome window.
PlaySense.Core · PlaySense.Editor

          
Movement
Paths, speed, idle time.
PlaySenseMovementModule

          
Attention
What players look at.
PlaySense.Attention

          
Performance
FPS, lows, hitches.
PlaySense.Performance

          
Replay
Scene-view playback.
PlaySense.Replay.Editor

          
Reports
PDF export.
PlaySense.Reports.Editor

          
Yours
Health, stamina, economy: anything.
class MyModule : PlaySenseModule

        

      

    

  


  
  

    

      

        
Your data stays yours.
Sessions are plain JSON files on your disk. Nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you decide to.

        
Safe to ship.
Event calls do nothing when PlaySense isn't recording, so they can stay in your release build.

        
Free forever.
MIT licensed and open source. Use it in commercial games, change it, make it yours.
